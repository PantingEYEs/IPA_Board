IPA Board modifications: no changes to the upstream files listed above.
bopomofo.custom.yaml disables user learning and automatic completion and selects traditional output.
terra_pinyin.custom.yaml applies the same learning/completion policy and traditional output.
The pinned Terra schema accepts - / < \\ for tones 1-4, with ; , . as optional alternatives.
Its spelling algebra does not accept the > fourth-tone alternative mentioned in the upstream README.
Candidate conversion to simplified Chinese is performed by IPA Board's existing OpenCC integration.
